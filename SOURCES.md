# Data and dependency sources

This file records the main external resources under consideration. Inclusion here does not imply that their entire datasets or content have been copied into this repository.

- chess.js: https://github.com/jhlywa/chess.js — chess rules and move validation; BSD-2-Clause.
- Lichess open database: https://database.lichess.org/ — games, puzzles, and evaluations; review the specific dataset license and current terms before downloading or redistributing.
- Lichess opening names: https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings — opening names and move sequences; CC0 dataset.
- Stockfish: https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish — chess engine; GPLv3. Distribution requires attention to the license and corresponding source requirements.
- FIDE Laws of Chess: https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/E012023 — reference for official over-the-board rules.

The current starter exercises and lesson summaries are original examples written for ChessForge. Do not reproduce proprietary lessons, books, puzzle explanations, or branded material from other chess platforms.
